What drives kitchen remodeling prices up or down

Pushes costs up

  • Moving plumbing, gas, or electrical (layout changes)
  • Custom or semi-custom cabinetry
  • Stone countertops
  • Premium appliance packages

Keeps costs down

  • Cabinet refacing instead of replacement
  • Keeping the existing layout
  • Laminate or butcher-block counters
  • Phasing the project
How to read these numbers. These ranges are compiled from published cost guides (Angi, HomeAdvisor, HomeGuide, and regional contractor sources) — not from jobs EstimateGuard has observed. They show what the guides say a fair price looks like in Philadelphia, PA; your actual quotes will vary with the specifics of your project.
