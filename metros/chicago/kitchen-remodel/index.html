What drives the price up or down

Pushes costs up

  • Moving plumbing, gas, or electrical (layout changes)
  • Custom or semi-custom cabinetry
  • Stone countertops and high-end appliances
  • Structural work (removing walls, new openings)

Keeps costs down

  • Cosmetic refresh: paint, hardware, refacing
  • Keeping the existing layout
  • Stock cabinets and laminate counters
  • Phasing the work instead of a full gut at once
How to read these numbers. These ranges are compiled from published cost guides (Angi, HomeAdvisor, HomeGuide, and regional contractor sources) — not from jobs EstimateGuard has observed. They show what the guides say a fair price looks like in Chicago, IL; your actual quotes will vary with the specifics of your project.
