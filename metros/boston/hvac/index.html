What drives the price up or down

Pushes costs up

  • Full system replacement vs. a single component
  • Ductwork replacement or additions
  • High-efficiency equipment
  • Difficult access (tight attic or crawlspace)
  • Electrical panel work to support new equipment

Keeps costs down

  • Like-for-like equipment swaps
  • Existing ductwork in good condition
  • Standard-efficiency units
  • Easily accessible installation location
How to read these numbers. These ranges are compiled from published cost guides (Angi, HomeAdvisor, HomeGuide, and regional contractor sources) — not from jobs EstimateGuard has observed. They show what the guides say a fair price looks like in Boston, MA; your actual quotes will vary with the specifics of your project. A missing value means the guides didn't publish one, not that the cost is zero. Full methodology.
