What drives the price up or down

Pushes costs up

  • Steep pitch or multiple stories
  • Tearing off old layers before installing
  • Premium materials (metal, tile, slate)
  • Structural deck repairs underneath
  • Skylights, chimneys, and flashing details

Keeps costs down

  • Simple gable roof, single story
  • Standard architectural asphalt shingles
  • Overlay over one existing layer (where code allows)
  • Scheduling in the off-season
How to read these numbers. These ranges are compiled from published cost guides (Angi, HomeAdvisor, HomeGuide, and regional contractor sources) — not from jobs EstimateGuard has observed. They show what the guides say a fair price looks like in Miami, FL; your actual quotes will vary with the specifics of your project. A missing value means the guides didn't publish one, not that the cost is zero. Full methodology.
