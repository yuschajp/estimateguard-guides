What drives hvac prices up or down

Pushes costs up

  • Full system replacement vs. a single component
  • High-efficiency (high-SEER) equipment
  • Ductwork repair or replacement
  • Difficult equipment access (attic, crawlspace)
  • Permit and refrigerant handling

Keeps costs down

  • Single-component repairs
  • Like-for-like equipment swaps
  • Easily accessible units
  • Regular maintenance plans
How to read these numbers. These ranges are compiled from published cost guides (Angi, HomeAdvisor, HomeGuide, and regional contractor sources) — not from jobs EstimateGuard has observed. They show what the guides say a fair price looks like in Nashville, TN; your actual quotes will vary with the specifics of your project. A missing value means the guides didn't publish one, not that the cost is zero. Full methodology.
