What drives roofing prices up or down

Pushes costs up

  • Steep or complex rooflines
  • Tear-off of multiple old layers
  • Premium materials (metal, tile, slate)
  • Wind/hurricane-code requirements
  • Difficult access or multi-story homes

Keeps costs down

  • Simple gable rooflines
  • Standard architectural shingles
  • Single-layer tear-off
  • Scheduling in the off-season
How to read these numbers. These ranges are compiled from published cost guides (Angi, HomeAdvisor, HomeGuide, and regional contractor sources) — not from jobs EstimateGuard has observed. They show what the guides say a fair price looks like in San Diego, CA; your actual quotes will vary with the specifics of your project.
