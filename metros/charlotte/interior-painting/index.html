What drives interior painting prices up or down

Pushes costs up

  • High or vaulted ceilings
  • Heavy prep (patching, wallpaper removal)
  • Premium paints and finishes
  • Detailed trim and millwork

Keeps costs down

  • Single rooms in good condition
  • Walls needing minimal prep
  • Standard-quality paints
  • Bundling multiple rooms
How to read these numbers. These ranges are compiled from published cost guides (Angi, HomeAdvisor, HomeGuide, and regional contractor sources) — not from jobs EstimateGuard has observed.
