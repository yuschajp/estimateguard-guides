What drives electrical prices up or down

Pushes costs up

  • Panel or service upgrades needing a utility disconnect
  • Whole-home rewiring
  • Trenching or long outdoor runs
  • Permit and inspection fees

Keeps costs down

  • Single fixture, switch, or outlet swaps
  • An accessible, modern panel
  • Bundling several small jobs into one visit
  • Surface-mounted raceways where allowed
How to read these numbers. These ranges are compiled from published cost guides (Angi, HomeAdvisor, HomeGuide, and regional contractor sources) — not from jobs EstimateGuard has observed. They show what the guides say a fair price looks like in Charlotte, NC; your actual quotes will vary with the specifics of your project.
