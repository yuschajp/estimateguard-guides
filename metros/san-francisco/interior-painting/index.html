What drives the price up or down

Pushes costs up

  • High or vaulted ceilings
  • Heavy prep: patching, sanding, wallpaper removal
  • Premium paints and multiple colors
  • Trim, doors, and ceilings included
  • Occupied home (furniture moving and masking)

Keeps costs down

  • Empty rooms with minimal prep
  • Walls only, single color
  • Standard-quality paints
How to read these numbers. These ranges are compiled from published cost guides (Angi, HomeAdvisor, HomeGuide, and regional contractor sources) — not from jobs EstimateGuard has observed. They show what the guides say a fair price looks like in San Francisco, CA; your actual quotes will vary with the specifics of your project. A missing value means the guides didn't publish one, not that the cost is zero. Full methodology.
